**Subject:** Permission request — republishing deal-level Arms Transfers data on a non-commercial research site

Dear SIPRI Arms Transfers team,

My name is [FULL NAME], based in [CITY, COUNTRY] [and affiliated with ORGANISATION / "an independent researcher"].
I have built a free, non-commercial research website that uses the SIPRI Arms Transfers Database to estimate, for
each importing country, which countries designed the major weapons it still operates — and therefore which
suppliers it depends on for spare parts, upgrades and munitions. It is aimed at analysts.

The site currently publishes only my own estimates and aggregates, with attribution ("Information from the
Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (SIPRI), https://www.sipri.org/databases/armstransfers").

I would like to also show, on each country page, the individual deal records behind each estimate (recipient,
supplier, designation, weapon description, order year, delivery years, units, TIV and your comments), so that
readers can verify every figure. As this would reproduce more than 10% of the dataset, I am asking for your
permission. I would:

- attribute SIPRI on every page and link each country to your database;
- not charge for access, sell the data, or offer a bulk download of your records;
- update from your official release each year and state the release used;
- remove the records promptly if you ask.

The site is at [URL once live]. I would be glad to share anything else you need, and to follow any conditions you set.
